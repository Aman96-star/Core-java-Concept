Two Pointer technique is a DSA approach where we use two variables (pointers) to keep track of two positions in an array or string.
Instead of using extra arrays or repeatedly scanning elements, we move the two pointers toward each other or in the same direction.
1. Basic idea
For an array:
-------------------------------
[10, 20, 30, 40, 50]
 ↑              ↑
left           right

-------------------------------

int left = 0;
int right = arr.length - 1;

------------------------------

Step 1:
[10, 20, 30, 40, 50]
 ↑              ↑
left           right

Step 2:
[50, 20, 30, 40, 10]
     ↑        ↑
    left     right

Step 3:
[50, 40, 30, 20, 10]
         ↑  ↑
        left right

-------------------------------
